STEP 1

Upload your reports

Choose a report tab and load the files for the dates you want to review.

1
Upload production dashboard

Use the DFOS export with daily machine output, plan, rejects, and uptime. One file can contain several days. This report can be reviewed on its own.

What columns should it contain?

Date, Machine Name, Plan Production, Actual Production. Model, rejections, uptime, and downtime are also used when present.

2
Upload detailed downtime report

Choose one or more files with machine, date, downtime minutes, and recorded reason. This report can also be reviewed on its own.

What columns should it contain?

Date, Machine Name, downtime minutes, and Downtime Reason. Hour, planned/unplanned minutes, and remarks are also used when present.

3
Upload rejection report

Choose one or more rejection reports. Production quantity, rejected quantity, and the recorded rejection reason are used for the rate and reason charts.

What columns should it contain?

Date, Machine Name, Model/SKU, Production, Rejection Qty, and Rejection Reasons (In Gram). Hour, Shift, Rejection Weight, and Remark are also used when present.

When can the reports be connected?Date mismatches are okay for separate reviews. To compare rejection and downtime records, the date, hour, machine, and model should match.
