🔴 The Challenge (Before)
Manual Friction

Plant monitors display live DFOS screens, but daily operational reviews required tedious manual synthesis across 3 separate workbooks:

  • Fragmented Files: Production, Detailed Downtime, and Scrap Rejections lived in disconnected sheets.
  • Arithmetic Glitches: 22 rows had OEE > 100% (up to 209%), and downtime fields failed mathematical reconciliation.
  • Delayed Root-Cause Analysis: Supervisors spent 90+ minutes copy-pasting numbers instead of investigating shop-floor fixes.
🟢 The Intervention (After)
Smart Automation

A zero-install, decoupled workspace unifying all 3 reports with instant calculations and bounded AI explanation:

  • Unified Ingestion: In-browser Drag & Drop harmonizes files by Date, Machine Cell, Model, and Shift.
  • Deterministic Paretos: Code automatically computes Top 10 Downtime & Scrap rankings before AI sees it.
  • Closed-Loop Actions: Observed losses link directly to RCA cases, assigned owners, and 30-day recurrence audits.
